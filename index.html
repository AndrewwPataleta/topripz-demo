The problem

Sleeves were the safest part of the trip. Until they weren't.

Every major grading service requires you to ship cards in a clear, semi-rigid sleeve. It's a sensible rule — the sleeve protects the card and lets the grader inspect it.

But the sleeves themselves are tight. Cards stick. Pulling them out is where bends, scratches, and dented corners happen — and a single dinged edge can drop a grade and thousands of dollars off the card's value.

Topripz keeps everything the graders require, and removes the part that damages the card.

FAQ

The questions collectors actually ask.

Can't find your answer? support@topripz.com

Why not just use a regular semi-rigid?
Regular semi-rigid sleeves are tight by design — they have to be, to hold the card flush. That tightness is what causes friction damage during extraction. Topripz keeps the same protective standard, but adds a tear-tab so the sleeve opens around the card instead of dragging against it.
Which grading services accept Topripz?
Accepted as a submission supply by all major US graders — including PSA, BGS, SGC, CGC, and TAG. The sleeve meets the standard clear, semi-rigid requirement that each service publishes in their submission guidelines.
Will the tear-tab open accidentally in transit?
No. The perforation requires a deliberate pull — single-pass tear, two-finger grip. It doesn't separate from incidental pressure, drops, or shipping handling. We've stress-tested through standard USPS, UPS, and FedEx flows.
What card sizes does it fit?
Standard trading card size, up to 75pt thickness — covering modern and vintage cardboard, plus thicker relic and patch cards.
